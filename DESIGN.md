## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial, sensual, and fluid atmosphere crafted for high-end dance artists, choreographers, and movement academies. Grounded in deep stage blacks, incandescent magenta-pinks, and ethereal purples, the aesthetic bridges the discipline of classical conservatory dance with the boldness of urban heels, street jazz, and commercial choreography.

The design movement combines **High-Contrast Editorial** with subtle **Glassmorphism**. Key characteristics include:
- Deep, theatrical dark canvases reminiscent of black-box performance halls.
- Sophisticated, high-contrast serif typography paired with structured, architectural sans-serif micro-copy.
- Translucent scrims, glowing gradient backdrops, and luminous borders that echo stage lighting and ribbon-like movement.
- Expressive motion curves and generous spatial breathing room to celebrate the physical form and motion capture.

## Colors

The palette operates primarily in an unapologetic deep dark mode. Pure obsidian black (`#000000`) serves as the void of the stage floor, contrasted against brilliant white and soft warm alabaster neutrals for flawless editorial legibility.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C2185B` - Dunkelrosa / Deep Magenta):** Used for focal calls-to-action, active highlights, and vibrant glowing states.
- **Secondary (`#8E44AD` - Lila / Royal Stage Violet):** Used for secondary accents, atmospheric gradient blends, and event category tags.
- **Tertiary (`#C77D9B` - Soft Rose):** A subdued, dusty rose utilized for subtle hover fills, badge containers, and secondary text accents.
- **Neutral Dark (`#000000` & `#0D0B0E`):** Surfaces, modal canvases, and deep layout containers.
- **Neutral Light (`#FFFFFF` & `#F5F2F4`):** Crisp headline typography and silky high-contrast body text.

### Gradient Accents
Linear transitions blending `#C2185B` to `#8E44AD` at a 135-degree angle mimic dynamic neon ribbon illumination, applied to decorative rules, premium buttons, and hero typography masks.

## Typography

The typography pairings intentionally balance classical artistic drama with contemporary technical precision:
- **Headings & Hero Statements (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes the prestige of the Paris Conservatory, haute couture, and theatrical bills. It brings dramatic high contrast between thick stems and thin hairlines.
- **Subheadings, Body, and Functional UI (`Montserrat`):** Provides a geometric, modern, and highly legible counterpart. Montserrat SemiBold anchors section headers, Regular guarantees effortless reading in editorial bio blocks, and Medium delivers crisp, deliberate clarity across buttons, navigation links, and schedule chips.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a 4-column layout on mobile devices.
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (2.5rem), `margin-lg` (4rem) to maintain expansive stage margins and cinematic negative space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem), `margin` (2rem).
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (1rem), `margin-sm` (1rem).

Layout rhythm is generous and vertical. Full-width visual blocks, asymmetric split-screens (e.g., text paired against large portrait imagery), and sticky booking floating bars command structural clarity while allowing dance media to dominate without clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is delivered via tonal surface layering, frosted glass backdrops, and colored luminous auras rather than conventional muddy drop shadows:

1. **Base Surface (Stage Level 0):** Pure black `#000000`.
2. **Elevated Surface (Level 1):** `#110D13` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Glassmorphic Overlays (Level 2):** `rgba(20, 15, 24, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an ultra-fine perimeter line `rgba(194, 24, 91, 0.25)`.
4. **Active Dramatic Glows:** Interactive and featured items cast diffused illumination (`box-shadow: 0 0 32px -4px rgba(194, 24, 91, 0.35)` or `0 0 24px -2px rgba(142, 68, 173, 0.3)`), invoking spotlights and neon studio mirrors.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, refined geometry (`roundedness: 1`).
- **Base Components (Inputs, Cards, Badges):** Feature gentle 4px (`0.25rem`) to 8px (`0.5rem`) radiuses. This avoids overly playful bubble aesthetics, preserving a sleek, editorial edge.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Reservation chips, level indicator badges, and primary action buttons can employ complete capsule shapes (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular editorial photography grids and mirror the fluid arcs of choreography.
- **Imagery:** Hero portraits and video thumbnails incorporate either crisp square proportions or gentle 8px borders accompanied by subtle gradient borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Workshop / Reserve Class):** Gradient fill transitioning from `#C2185B` to `#8E44AD` at 135deg. Text in white `#FFFFFF`, `label-lg`, uppercase with slight tracking (`0.06em`). Soft glow on hover with scale transition (`transform: scale(1.02)`).
- **Secondary Action (View Portfolio / Style Details):** Transparent background, 1px solid border in `#C77D9B`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions to a soft overlay `rgba(199, 125, 155, 0.15)`.
- **Text Link:** `#F5F2F4` with a thin gradient underline that expands from center on hover.

### Cards (Course & Workshop Cards)
- Background surface of `#110D13` with a 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Integrated aspect-ratio image container (3:4 portrait or 16:9 video preview) capped with category tag.
- Typography: Course level in `label-sm` (`#C77D9B`), class title in `headline-sm` (`#FFFFFF`), style description in `body-md` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`).

### Level & Style Chips
- Compact pills with `rgba(142, 68, 173, 0.15)` fill and `rgba(142, 68, 173, 0.4)` border.
- Text rendered in `label-md` in `#FFFFFF` (e.g., "Débutant", "Intermédiaire / Avancé", "Street Jazz", "Heels").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Dark fields (`#0D0B0E`) with 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- On focus: Border illuminates to `#C2185B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(194, 24, 91, 0.3)`). Placeholder in muted taupe-gray `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.

### Lists & FAQs
- Accordion lists separating class pedagogy rounds (e.g., "1er round", "2e round", "3e round").
- Border-bottom dividers colored `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Numeric markers styled with `Playfair Display` italic in `#C2185B`.

### Navigation Bar
- Floating glassmorphic dock (`rgba(10, 8, 12, 0.8)` with blur `20px`).
- Monogram/Wordmark centered or left-aligned with glowing pink-purple ligature accents.
